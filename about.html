me

The non-work stuff.

1 Hi, I'm Miguel

If I'm not working, there's a good chance I'm playing football (soccer, for my American friends), watching it, or arguing about it. It's been part of my life for as long as I can remember.

I also run a lot. Mostly to clear my head, sometimes to justify a second dinner. You can follow along, or judge my pace, on Strava ↗.

I'm a map person. I can lose an hour on Google Earth without noticing, and I read about geopolitics the way some people read novels: why a border sits where it does, who controls the water, why a tiny port suddenly matters to everyone.

Which is probably why I love traveling. New city, no real plan, comfortable shoes, and I'm happy. When I'm home, art scratches the same itch: I'm regularly at one of New York's museums, or catching something at Instituto Cervantes New York.
